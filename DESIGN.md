
# ትግል ለ30: The Struggle for 30

## 1. Project Overview

An AI-generated short film inspired by a real-life
taxi journey from Bulbula to Bole in Addis Ababa,
Ethiopia.

The film follows a taxi driver struggling to earn
his daily income while operating an aging and
mechanically unreliable minibus.

## 2. Creative Intent

The film aims to reveal the often-overlooked
physical and emotional struggles of public
transport drivers in Addis Ababa.

Rather than focusing on fare disagreements,
the film prioritizes the driver's determination,
physical effort, mechanical difficulties and
persistence.

The central message is that an ordinary journey
for a passenger can represent a daily struggle
for someone else.

## 3. Target Audience

Primary audience:
- Addis Ababa public transport users
- Young Ethiopian adults
- Working professionals and daily commuters

Secondary audience:
- Ethiopian diaspora
- International audiences interested in
  Ethiopian urban culture

## 4. Intended Audience Response

The audience should:
- Recognize familiar aspects of Addis Ababa life.
- Develop empathy for public transport drivers.
- Reflect on the unseen effort behind everyday
  services.
- Understand the significance of thirty birr
  within the context of the story.

## 5. Storytelling Approach

The story is told from the first-person
perspective of a passenger.

The driver is the main character.
The aging taxi functions as a secondary character.

Mechanical struggles form the central conflict.
Fare disagreements provide supporting context.

The film avoids assigning blame to either
the driver or the passengers.

## 6. Visual Direction

Style: Cinematic social realism.

Color palette:
- Faded blue
- Weathered white
- Dusty earth tones
- Natural morning sunlight

Camera approach:
- Observational documentary framing
- Close-ups of mechanical controls
- Close-ups of the driver's hands
- Restrained camera movement
- Natural facial expressions
- Passenger point-of-view shots

The visual style must preserve the dignity
of the driver and avoid exaggerated poverty.

## 7. Audio Direction

Narration: Conversational Amharic.
Subtitles: English.

Environmental sounds:
- Addis Ababa street ambience
- Engine ignition and stalling
- Mechanical rattling
- Gear shifting
- Footsteps
- Physical exertion
- Passenger calls

Music:
Original Ethiopian-inspired instrumental music
with subtle krar-like textures and percussion.

Music should remain secondary to the narration
and environmental sounds.

## 8. Technical Specifications

Duration: 88 seconds
Storyboard: 11 shots, including the end card
Primary aspect ratio: 9:16
Secondary aspect ratio: 16:9
Output format: MP4

Image generation: Cloudflare Workers AI
Video generation: Wan 2.2
Narration: Gemini TTS
Music generation: ACE-Step
Assembly: FFmpeg

## 9. Ethical Considerations

The story is based on the author's experience.

All depicted characters will have fictional
appearances. No identifiable likeness or voice
of an actual driver or passenger will be used
without informed consent.

The film will include a visible AI disclosure.

## 10. Success Criteria

- The audience identifies the driver's struggle
  as the main theme.
- The mechanical difficulties feel believable.
- The taxi and driver remain visually consistent.
- The narration sounds natural.
- The English subtitles are readable.
- The story remains faithful to the actual event.
- The complete film can be reproduced through
  an automated pipeline.
